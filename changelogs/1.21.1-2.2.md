# Release 2.2 - Snapshot 1

## Gameplay

* Tombs
  * Now come in 16 stone types: Tomb, Darkslate, Aridrock, Glist, Grimestone, Andesite, Granite, Basalt, Blackstone, Diorite, Tuff, Deepslate, Calcite, Dripstone, Prismarine, and Dark Prismarine.
* Tomb lids
  * Can be opened from either side.
  * Stone impact sounds play when the lid hits the ground as it opens and when it slams shut.
* Tomb beds
  * Tombs can be used as beds.
  * Sleeping in a tomb prevents phantom spawns.
* Death Anchors
  * Each resurrection halves maximum health, but you cannot fall below one heart.
  * After a 60-second delay, maximum health recovers by one heart per minute until restored.
* String Lights
  * Hanging light strings attach to block faces, connect together, and emit light.
  * Available in 16 dye colors.
* Darkslate heating
  * Darkslate slabs, stairs, walls, and vertical slabs are now heatable.
  * Polished Darkslate and Darkslate Bricks, plus their available slab, stair, wall, and vertical slab forms, are heatable too.
  * Chiseled and cracked Darkslate Bricks are also heatable.

## Glowshroom Forest Overhaul

### General changes

* Glowshroom wood set
  * Added as a new wood type, with planks, boats, chest boats, signs, and hanging signs.
  * Glowshroom wood blocks glow in the dark.
* Glist
  * A new bluish stone type in the Glowshroom Forest.
* Shelf glowshrooms
  * New in the Glowshroom Forest; they speed up crop growth of the crop on top of the block they are attached to.
  * Bone meal grows a small shelf glowshroom into a larger one.
* Gulbs
  * New ceiling-growing plants that give off a faint glow.
* Glowspurs
  * They now poison creatures they catch in addition to slowing them. Glowshroom Monsters are immune.
* Mossy Grimestone
  * New mossy blocks, plus matching bricks, stairs, slabs, and walls.

### Glowing Mycoses

* Glowshroom Cap
  * No longer grants constant Haste. While worn, it grants Glowing Mycoses instead.
* Glowing Mycoses
  * Speeds up crops when you stand on farmland.
  * Eating rotten flesh, defeating undead, or entering range of a glowshroom heart while affected will trigger the active health boost.
  * Rotten flesh while under the effect of Glowing Mycoses is a substantially better food source.
  * This effect is experimental, and player feedback is welcome.

## Sandy Catacombs Overhaul

* Sandy Catacombs terrain
  * New Dry Dirt now generates in the caves.
  * New pointed Aridrock formations grow from the floor and ceiling.
  * Petrified branch and root features have been reworked, with updated shapes in the caves.

## Molten Cavern

* Scorchers
  * Fixed an issue that caused them to spawn on the surface.
  * Now search for players and lock on after spotting them.
  * Sprinting within 32 blocks can alert them even through cover. Walking or crouching does not alert them by sound.
* Scorched Remains
  * Recipes now make two at a time.
  * One item smelts 5 items, down from 10; one block smelts 12, down from 24.
* Darkslate
  * At heat level 4, it damages entities that walk over it, like a magma block.
  * Sneaking prevents this damage.

## Visual updates

* Glowshroom visuals
  * Glowshroom, Glowshroom Stem, the Glowshroom Heart, and Glimmering Vines have updated models and textures.
  * The Glowshroom Monster has an updated model and texture.
  * Glowspurs have a new texture.
  * The Glowshroom Lantern model has been overhauled.
* Grimestone and Duskrock
  * Grimestone's palette is now bluer. Grimestone Bricks and Mossy Sprouts have updated textures.
  * Duskrock's texture has changed slightly, and Polished Duskrock has a new texture.
* Sandy Catacombs textures
  * Dry Sprouts and Amber Cluster textures have been updated.
* Fog visibility
  * The Paranoia Altar's glowing texture is now visible through fog.
  * Glowing parts of Void Souls, Void Soul Knights, and Body Snatchers can now be seen through fog.

## Emissive blocks

* Heated Darkslate
  * At heat levels 2–4, the base Darkslate texture now glows faintly.
* Mossy Grimestone
  * Mossy Grimestone and Mossy Grimestone Bricks now have subtle glowing details.

## New sounds

* Geysers & Scorchers have new sounds.
* Void Soul Knights have new sounds for their upward and downward sword swings.

## Configuration

* You can now disable the Glowshroom Forest, Sandy Catacombs, or Molten Cavern from generating with individual config options.
* A config option lets you turn off Darker Depths' custom shaders if they cause problems with shader packs.

## Fixed issues

* Fixed an issue where regular mineshafts could generate in the same locations as Petrified Mineshafts in the Sandy Catacombs and Molten Cavern.


## Known issues

* Glowshroom Monster
  * Its behavior, model, and animations are still being refined and may not always look or act as intended.
* Glowshroom Forest mechanics
  * Several features are still being tested and may change as they are refined. Looking for feedback!
