# Release 2.2 - Snapshot 2

## Gameplay

* String Lights
  * All 16 colors now have crafting recipes.
  * Three string, two Glow Grime, and one matching dye make six String Lights.
* Dry Dirt
  * Can now be crafted with two dirt and one sand to make three Dry Dirt.
  * Using a hoe turns it into regular dirt.
  * Bone meal grows Dry Sprouts on top, with a chance to grow more on nearby Dry Dirt.
* Dry Sprouts
  * Can now be placed on sand.

## Glowshroom Forest

* Glowing Mycoses
  * I have scrapped this for this update, and will return to the idea and fleshing this out in the future. Glowshroom Cap now gives haste again and the Glowshroom Heart no longer is a block entity.
* Glowspurs
  * No longer generate and are hidden from the creative tab.
  * Existing Glowspurs remain, with the same legacy tooltip as Ash blocks.
* Gulbs
  * Removed the block and its assets.

## Sandy Catacombs

* Surface patches
  * New patches generate in deserts and savannas above Sandy Catacombs.
  * Desert patches contain Aridrock and Dry Dirt, with Dry Sprouts and occasional Pointed Aridrock.
  * Savanna patches contain Dry Dirt and Duskrock, with Dry Sprouts.
* Arid boulders
  * Now use Duskrock instead of Dry Dirt between the Aridrock layers.

## Visual updates

* Void Soul Torches
  * Updated the flame and smoke particles, glowing effect, and item texture.
  * The glowing parts are now visible through fog.
* Death Anchors
  * Powered Death Anchors have new glowing textures, including an animated top.
  * The glow is now visible through fog.
* Duskrock
  * Updated the remaining Duskrock Pillar and Tomb textures to match the new palette.
* Glowshroom Heart
  * Updated its texture.

## Performance

* Darkslate heating
  * Heating now prioritizes chunks near players within simulation distance.
  * Various other small improvements to the system.

## Configuration

* Default heat baking time budget has increased from 1 to 5 milliseconds.
